## What I Understood

### Why Preprocessing Matters

- Raw images often have noise, uneven lighting, or unwanted artefacts
- Preprocessing cleans and prepares the image so later steps (detection, segmentation) work correctly
- Garbage in, garbage out — if the input image is noisy, any further analysis will be unreliable
- A typical preprocessing pipeline: load → resize → convert to grayscale → reduce noise → enhance

### Types of Noise

- **Gaussian noise** — random intensity variations across the image, looks like a uniform grain
- **Salt-and-pepper noise** — random black and white pixels scattered across the image
- **Poisson noise** — caused by low light conditions; more pronounced in dark regions
- Noise comes from camera sensors, transmission errors, or low light conditions

### Convolution and Kernels

- A **kernel** (also called a filter or mask) is a small matrix, typically 3×3 or 5×5
- **Convolution** slides this kernel over every pixel in the image
- At each position, the kernel values are multiplied with the pixel values underneath, and the results are summed to give the new pixel value
- The kernel shape determines the effect: smoothing, sharpening, edge detection, etc.

### Mean Filter

- Replaces each pixel with the **average** of all pixels in its neighbourhood
- Simple and fast
- Blurs the image and reduces Gaussian noise
- Downside: also blurs edges, losing important boundary information

### Gaussian Filter

- Similar to mean filter but uses **weighted averaging** — pixels closer to the centre get higher weights
- Weight values follow the shape of a Gaussian (bell) curve
- Smoother result than mean filter; better at preserving some edge detail
- Commonly used before edge detection to remove noise first

### Median Filter

- Replaces each pixel with the **median value** of its neighbourhood (not the average)
- Very effective at removing **salt-and-pepper noise**
- Key advantage: it preserves edges much better than mean or Gaussian filters
- Slightly slower than linear filters but often worth it for noisy images
